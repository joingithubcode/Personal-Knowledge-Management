# resume-best-practices

## Purpose

Explain what makes a resume effective for software roles.

## Context

A resume has a few seconds to earn attention from a reviewer, human or
automated. It must communicate fit quickly: what the person did, what they
achieved, and whether their skills match the role. The format and wording
decide whether that message lands.

## Main Notes

- Lead with the facts reviewers scan for: role target, skills, and relevant
  experience.
- Describe experience with actions and outcomes, not vague duties.
- Use numbers where possible: scope, impact, and scale make claims concrete.
- Tailor the resume to each role, highlighting the skills the job asks for.
- Use standard section names and simple formatting so automated systems can
  parse the content.
- Keep it to one page for most experience levels; brevity is a feature.
- Skills should be specific and honest; listing tools you cannot use fails
  in interviews.
- Proofread for errors and keep the file in a widely accepted format.
