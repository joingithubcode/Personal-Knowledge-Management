# Artificial-Intelligence

Knowledge about artificial intelligence as a field.

## Purpose

Capture durable understanding of AI concepts, methods, and applications
that remain useful as the field evolves.

## Scope

- Core AI concepts and paradigms.
- Agents, reasoning, and knowledge representation.
- Applied AI methods and their trade-offs.

## Folder Contents

- One atomic Markdown note per topic.
- Each note registered in the repository INDEX.md.

## When to use

- When capturing a durable AI concept or method.
- When generalizing a lesson beyond one tool or model.
- When understanding of AI guides future learning.
