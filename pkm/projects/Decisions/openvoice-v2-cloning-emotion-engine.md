# openvoice-v2-cloning-emotion-engine

## Purpose

Record the choice of OpenVoice V2 as the voice cloning and emotion engine.

## Context

The platform needed a voice cloning and emotion engine that runs on CPU and
carries a permissive license. OpenVoice V2 met both requirements, unlike
XTTS-v2.

## Main Notes

- OpenVoice V2 (MIT licensed) was chosen as the voice cloning and emotion
  engine.
- It is CPU-optimized.
- It has a permissive license.
- This contrasts with XTTS-v2, which has commercial-use restrictions.
