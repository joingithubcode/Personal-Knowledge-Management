# Meeting Notes Template

## Purpose

Record what was discussed and decided in a single project meeting, so
the outcome is recoverable without a tool.

## Context

Describe the project, the meeting date, the attendees, and the meeting's
goal.

## Main Notes

- List each agenda item discussed, one bullet per item.
- Record decisions reached, one bullet per decision.
- Record action items with owner and due date.
- Keep every bullet short and factual.

## References

- List documents reviewed or shared during the meeting.
- Cite title and source when known.
