# Decision Log Template

## Purpose

Record a single project decision, its rationale, and its alternatives, so
choices remain explainable over time.

## Context

Describe the project and the situation that required the decision.

## Main Notes

- State the decision in one clear sentence.
- List the alternatives considered and why each was rejected.
- Note who decided and when.
- Record the expected impact of the choice.

## References

- List sources or documents that informed the decision.
- Cite title and source when known.
