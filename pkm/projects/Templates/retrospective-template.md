# Retrospective Template

## Purpose

Capture what went well and what to improve for a finished project phase,
so lessons feed the next phase.

## Context

Describe the project, the phase reviewed, and the people involved.

## Main Notes

- List what went well, one bullet per point.
- List what could be improved, one bullet per point.
- Record concrete actions for the next phase.
- Keep every bullet factual and non-personal.

## References

- List artifacts or data reviewed during the retrospective.
- Cite title and source when known.
