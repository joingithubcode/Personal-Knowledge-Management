# retraction-prediction-analysis

Project folder for analyzing the scientific method in ML research through the lens of retraction prediction.

Contains working notes, analysis records, and insights derived from Fletcher & Stevenson (2025) and related literature.

## Structure

- `analysis.md` — structured analysis of the research paper
- `INDEX.md` — note registry and organization plan
- `SUMMARY.md` — current note map

## Folder Mission

Capture the process of analysis: questions, sources, findings, and evolving understanding. Analysis notes are working documents that mature into knowledge notes.

## When to use

- When conducting structured analysis of a research paper
- When documenting findings and interpretations
- When tracking progress from draft to complete
