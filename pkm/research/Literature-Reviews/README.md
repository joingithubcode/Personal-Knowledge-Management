# Literature-Reviews

Notes synthesizing a body of related literature.

## Purpose

Capture durable syntheses of many sources: what a field or topic says as
a whole, where it agrees, and where it diverges.

## Scope

- Structured summaries of related papers and findings.
- Themes, gaps, and trends across a body of work.
- Conclusions drawn from reading broadly.

## Folder Contents

- One atomic Markdown note per review.
- Each note registered in the repository INDEX.md.

## When to use

- When a topic has been read widely enough to synthesize.
- When starting research in an unfamiliar area.
- When a field's landscape needs a stable record.
