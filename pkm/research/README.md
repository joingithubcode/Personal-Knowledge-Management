# research/

Active inquiries and working material in this personal knowledge repository.

## Purpose

Hold the process of learning: questions, sources, findings, and evolving
understanding. Research notes are working documents that mature into
knowledge notes.

## Scope

- Open questions and hypotheses under investigation.
- Collected sources, observations, and partial findings.
- Working notes that will grow, change, or be promoted.

## Folder Contents

- One atomic Markdown note per inquiry.
- Each note registered in the repository INDEX.md.
- One subfolder per category, each with a README.md and AGENTS.md.

## Categories

- [Papers](Papers/README.md) — notes about individual papers.
- [Literature-Reviews](Literature-Reviews/README.md) — syntheses of related literature.
- [Experiments](Experiments/README.md) — records of experiments.
- [Benchmarks](Benchmarks/README.md) — evaluation tasks and scoring.
- [Comparisons](Comparisons/README.md) — side-by-side comparisons.
- [Evaluations](Evaluations/README.md) — assessments of approaches.
- [Case-Studies](Case-Studies/README.md) — real-world instances.
- [Surveys](Surveys/README.md) — maps of fields and topics.
- [Notes](Notes/README.md) — general working research notes.
- [Open-Questions](Open-Questions/README.md) — unanswered research questions.

## When to use

- When starting to explore a new topic.
- When gathering material before understanding is complete.
- When working through an inquiry that is not yet settled.

## What does NOT belong here

- Finished, durable understanding ready for knowledge/.
- Permanent references that will never change.
- Random unconnected thoughts better suited to ideas/.
- Project-scoped material belonging in projects/.

## Related folders

- knowledge: the destination for mature research findings.
- ideas: raw starting points that may become research.
- projects: research scoped to one personal project.
