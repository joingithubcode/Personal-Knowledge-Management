# Notes

Working research notes that do not fit a more specific category.

## Purpose

Capture durable working notes from the research process: observations,
reasoning, and intermediate findings before they settle elsewhere.

## Scope

- One note per distinct research thread or observation.
- Working understanding that is not yet a paper or review.
- Material that links the other research categories.

## Folder Contents

- One atomic Markdown note per thread.
- Each note registered in the repository INDEX.md.

## When to use

- When research thinking needs a stable home.
- When a finding is real but not yet promoted.
- When a thread spans several specific categories.

## Related categories

- Papers
- Open-Questions
- Experiments
- Literature-Reviews
