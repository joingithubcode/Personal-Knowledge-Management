# Fletcher & Stevenson (2025) — Predicting Retracted Research

## Reference
Fletcher, A. H. A., & Stevenson, M. (2025). Predicting retracted research: a dataset and machine learning approaches. *Research Integrity and Peer Review, 10*(9). `https://doi.org/10.1186/s41073-025-00168-w`

## Links

## Metadata
- Publisher: Springer Nature
- Type: Peer-reviewed open-access research article
- Year: 2025
- Authors: Aaron H. A. Fletcher, Mark Stevenson (University of Sheffield)

## Summary
This study builds an open dataset combining Retraction Watch and OpenAlex, then trains and evaluates machine learning models to predict article retractions. It also performs an ablation study to assess feature importance.
