# Personal

Ideas about personal life, habits, and growth.

## Purpose

Capture and organize personal ideas early, so life and growth concepts
are not lost.

## Scope

- Life and lifestyle ideas.
- Habit and health concepts.
- Personal growth thoughts.

## Folder Contents

- One atomic Markdown note per idea.
- Each note registered in the repository INDEX.md.

## When to use

- When a personal idea appears.
- When a life concept deserves a record.
- When a growth thought needs a home until pursued.
