# ideas/

Raw thoughts and unformed ideas in this personal knowledge repository.

## Purpose

Provide a safe place to capture thoughts before they are developed. Ideas
are seeds: they are captured cheaply, reviewed, and either grown or
discarded.

## Scope

- Half-formed thoughts and sudden insights.
- Seeds of possible research or projects.
- Anything worth remembering but not yet understood.

## Folder Contents

- One atomic Markdown note per idea.
- Each note registered in the repository INDEX.md.
- One subfolder per category, each with a README.md and AGENTS.md.

## Categories

- [Business](Business/README.md) — business and venture ideas.
- [Software](Software/README.md) — software and tool ideas.
- [Artificial-Intelligence](Artificial-Intelligence/README.md) — AI concepts and applications.
- [Startups](Startups/README.md) — founding and venture ideas.
- [Content](Content/README.md) — creating and sharing content.
- [Learning](Learning/README.md) — study and skill ideas.
- [Career](Career/README.md) — professional direction ideas.
- [Personal](Personal/README.md) — personal life and growth ideas.
- [Future](Future/README.md) — long-term possibilities.
- [Archive](Archive/README.md) — ideas kept for the record.

## When to use

- When a thought appears and must not be lost.
- When an idea is too undeveloped for research or knowledge.
- When a possible project first takes shape.

## What does NOT belong here

- Finished knowledge or settled understanding.
- Active inquiries with sources and findings.
- Project-scoped content that is already underway.
- Action items, tasks, or schedules.

## Related folders

- research: where a developed idea begins its inquiry.
- projects: where an idea becomes an actual project.
- knowledge: the final home for grown understanding.
