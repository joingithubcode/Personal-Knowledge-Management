# Business

Ideas about business, products, and ventures.

## Purpose

Capture and organize business ideas early, so opportunities are not lost
before they are evaluated.

## Scope

- Product and service ideas.
- Business model and market thoughts.
- Venture and opportunity concepts.

## Folder Contents

- One atomic Markdown note per idea.
- Each note registered in the repository INDEX.md.

## When to use

- When a business or product idea appears.
- When a market or model thought is worth keeping.
- When an opportunity needs a home until evaluated.
