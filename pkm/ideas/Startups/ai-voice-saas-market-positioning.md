# ai-voice-saas-market-positioning

## Purpose

Preserve the original idea behind the AI Voice Platform: a Voice AI SaaS
competing with ElevenLabs and JSF Labs, targeting both the Pakistani and
international markets at the same time.

## Context

This idea predates the AI Voice Platform project. It records where the
project's market direction came from before development began.

## Main Notes

- The original idea was a Voice AI SaaS competing with ElevenLabs and
  JSF Labs.
- It would target the Pakistani market and international markets
  simultaneously, a dual-market positioning most competitors do not
  attempt together.
- The idea developed into the active project
  [[ai-voice-platform-overview]].
