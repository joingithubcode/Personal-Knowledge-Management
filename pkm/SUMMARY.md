# SUMMARY.md

A map of the entire Personal Knowledge Management repository. This file is
the entry point for humans and AI agents. Keep it current: every new note,
folder, or file must be registered here.

## Repository

- [README](README.md): Overview, purpose, and structure.
- [AGENTS](AGENTS.md): Working rules for editors and AI agents.
- [INDEX](INDEX.md): Master index of all notes.
- [GLOSSARY](GLOSSARY.md): Terms and abbreviations.
- [CHANGELOG](CHANGELOG.md): History of significant changes.
- [validation.yaml](validation.yaml): Machine-readable validation rules.

## System documents

- [STANDARDS](STANDARDS.md): Rules for notes and files.
- [WORKFLOW](WORKFLOW.md): How notes move through the lifecycle.
- [AI-GUIDE](AI-GUIDE.md): How AI agents read and update the repository.
- [CONTRIBUTING](CONTRIBUTING.md): Contribution workflow.

## Folders

- [knowledge](knowledge/): Permanent, well-organized knowledge.
- [research](research/): Active inquiries and source material.
- [projects](projects/): Knowledge attached to personal projects.
- [ideas](ideas/): Raw thoughts not yet developed.

## Note status life cycle

draft -> active -> complete -> archived

## Index maintenance

Every new note is registered in INDEX.md and listed in the matching
folder section above.
